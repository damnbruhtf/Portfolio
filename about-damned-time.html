The brief

Build the whole house—not just a room.

About Damned Time was a 13-person university studio production built around a haunted-house experience. I designed the complete level across four connected floors while leading the 3D team’s asset pipeline.

My role crossed visual and interactive boundaries: environment production, spatial pacing, startup and pause menus, character movement, shooting behavior, character-speed tuning and the sanity system.

Verified responsibilities

Ownership across art, level and systems.

Every responsibility below reflects work completed within the capstone team; no commercial shipping claim is implied.
