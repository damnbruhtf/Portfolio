Artist’s eye.

I’m a Toronto-based game creator focused on creepy, atmospheric worlds. My work moves between modeling, level design, real-time implementation, UI and gameplay—because the strongest environments are shaped by how they look, feel and play.

I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Interactive Arts and Science from Brock University, concentrating in 3D Art and Expression.
